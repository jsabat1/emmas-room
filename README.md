# Emma's Room

**Play the game here:**

## Overview
Emma's Room is an interactive fiction and environmental storytelling puzzle. Instead of relying on traditional exposition or dialogue, players uncover the mystery of a missing highschool student by inspecting the objects left behind in her dorm room. The narrative pieces together a story of academic burnout and a sudden, desperate escape.

## Features
*   **Mise-en-scène Storytelling:** Narrative delivered entirely through props and environment.
*   **Conditional Logic:** Player choices (like watering the abandoned plant) permanently alter the emotional tone of the ending.
*   **Progress Tracking:** Custom boolean variables ensure players must piece together all clues before unlocking the final sequence.

## Built With
*   Twine 2
*   Harlowe 3.3 Story Format
